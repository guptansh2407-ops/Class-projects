Mini Biography: Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India, was a prominent aerospace engineer and statesman. He served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007 and was the youngest of five siblings in a modest family.

Early Life

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam's father, Jainulabdeen, was a boat owner and imam of a local mosque, while his mother, Ashiamma, was a housewife. Despite the family's limited financial resources, they instilled in him values of hard work, honesty, and integrity.His early life was marked by a strong desire to learn and a passion for mathematics, which he pursued diligently. He completed his schooling at Schwartz Higher Secondary School in Ramanathapuram and later graduated in 1954 from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, with a degree in physics. His early years were marked by selling newspapers to support his family's income, which shaped his determination and work ethic.

Important Achievement

In 1997, he was awarded India's highest civilian honour, the Bharat Ratna, for his contributions to the scientific research and modernisation of defence technology in India.
